Philosophical DNA

The Creative Sparks Behind the Code.

Every project in this repository began not as an incremental benchmark tweak, but as a conceptual translation between distinct intellectual universes.

Act I • The Celestial Sphere

Astrophysical Standard Candles

How do astronomers gauge distance across billions of light years? They separate intrinsic stellar luminosity (photometric flux) from celestial coordinates. I realized neural vector representations do the exact opposite: they treat vector magnitude and angle as an undifferentiated mess. This spark yielded Project Milky Way.

Inspired: Project Milky Way → Hyperspherical Foliation
Act II • The Combat Psychology

The Myth of Artificial Difficulty

In modern gaming, bosses simulate difficulty by giving themselves 10,000,000 HP or reading controller inputs in 10ms. Once a player discovers an exploit loop ($A \to B \to C \to A$), the game breaks. I built Theseus' Foe so that bosses learn your psychological habits, forcing genuine tactical evolution rather than stat-checking.

Inspired: Theseus' Foe → Anti-Cyclic Markov RL
Act III • The Complex Plane

Orthogonality in the Complex Plane \(\mathbb{C}\)

When deep neural networks encounter regime shifts and market crashes, their gradients violently explode because trend and volatility fight for the same real-valued scalar weights. By lifting representations into the complex numbers (\(a + ib\)), the real component captures deterministic momentum while the imaginary component absorbs volatility spikes.

Inspired: CV-OSNS → Gradient Traffic Cop
Biomimicry & Spider Webs

SpiderNet Anomaly Detection

A spider senses struggling prey across tens of thousands of intersections not with sight, but through vibrational harmonic stress waves traveling through tensile silk. We modeled multi-shock graph anomalies as tensile stress residuals across Wasserstein topologies.

Classical Mechanics & Least Action

Reduction Network (GTD)

Hyperplane decision boundaries in Euclidean space are an uninspired hack. What if classification is viewed as physical kinetic action—the minimum energy required to transport a sample along neural vector fields to class prototype manifolds?

Embodied Reality & Time's Arrow

Flux: Zero-Rollback NLP

Transformers assume the universe allows infinite backward rewinds. But physical agents cannot un-speak words or un-drop fragile glasses. Flux bounds rational inference under zero-rollback irreversibility.

Project Milky Way: Hyperspherical Directional Codecs & Transverse Foliation

The Creative Genesis & Unified Triad:

Decomposes dense vector embeddings into photometric radial intensity (scalar distance) and hyperspherical directional bit-vectors, grounded in 3 unified formulations: Stiefel Grassmannian Submersion, Standard Candle Triangulation, and Harmonic Dimensional Multiplexing.

The Creative Genesis:

Reframes classification through the physical principle of least action. Rather than slicing Euclidean space with hyperplanes, GTD calculates the line-integral kinetic action required to transport a sample onto class prototype manifolds along parameterized velocity fields.

Background & Vision

About Anand (Anu) Srinivasan.

I am a Computer Science researcher and software systems engineer at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill. My passion lies at the intersection of non-Euclidean geometry, high-performance systems engineering, and adaptive intelligence.

Rather than viewing artificial intelligence as an exercise in scaling compute on naive Euclidean architectures, I look for deep, explanatory models in neighboring fields: how astrophysics measures the stars, how complex analysis separates phase from amplitude, how game designers craft combat tension, and how biological organisms navigate irreversible time.

Outside of research, I believe in writing bulletproof code: comprehensive unit test suites, sub-millisecond tail latency optimization, memory profiling, and reproducible scientific artifacts.
